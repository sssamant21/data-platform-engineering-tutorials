## Findings and remediation

- **Confirmed problem — Chapter 54:** A recommended `redis-benchmark` example offered `-c 50 -n 100000 -t get,set` without sufficient isolation/impact limits. Replaced with a strictly loopback-only, disposable instance smoke test: **2 clients, 200 requests**, explicit write/overwriting warning, stop gates, and variable expected output. The pattern scanner still correctly finds the *bounded* benchmark.
- **Confirmed problem — Chapters 03, 04, 18 and 40:** Incomplete command-fence examples showed bare `SCAN` (or `HSCAN/SSCAN/ZSCAN`) without required key/cursor arguments. Replaced with scoped lab patterns, cursor `0` and COUNT hints, plus expected cursor/result guidance; documented that results can be empty/duplicated and scanning is not a consistent snapshot. Chapter 69 was fixed previously.
- **Clarity issue — Chapters 12, 13 and 20:** Generic cache-key deletion was shown without sufficiently clear copy/paste boundaries. Added notes that keys are **conceptual**, ownership is required, and `DEL/UNLINK` return **1** for a removed key or **0** when absent.
- **Negative examples:** The 12 `KEYS *` line matches are prohibited/unsafe illustration examples already adjudicated in the baseline 81-pattern report; do **not** execute them in production. Several `DEL lock-key` and `DEL customer:1001` examples illustrate unsafe lock release, accidental deletion, or why backups matter.
- **Remainder:** Many deletion snippets target explicit `tutorial:chapterNN:...` keys, but **a prefix alone is not authorization**; others have placeholders or variables. Keep runtime and ownership review open. The ledger below distinguishes provisional categories, not blanket verified safety.

The categories here are **triage statuses**, not proof of command correctness or product-version compatibility. Context may extend beyond the excerpt. Official references: [SCAN](https://redis.io/docs/latest/commands/scan/), [UNLINK](https://redis.io/docs/latest/commands/unlink/), [DEL](https://redis.io/docs/latest/commands/del/), [KEYS](https://redis.io/docs/latest/commands/keys/), [Benchmark](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/oss_and_stack/management/optimization/benchmarks/).

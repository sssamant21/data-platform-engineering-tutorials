## Method and definitions

Each finding was reviewed against its surrounding headings and instructions, not just the regex. `NEGATIVE_GUIDANCE` means the document forbids the command or uses it as an anti-pattern/tabletop/restriction example; do not execute. `CAUTIONARY_ILLUSTRATION` means an insecure-looking command is intentionally used to explain a risk and is **not a preferred instruction**. `REMEDIATED_ACTIONABLE` denotes a copyable instruction that required a concrete change. The command snippets below never contain real credentials.

A pattern hit is not itself evidence of misuse. Official references: [FLUSHDB](https://redis.io/docs/latest/commands/flushdb/), [KEYS](https://redis.io/docs/latest/commands/keys/), [SCAN](https://redis.io/docs/latest/commands/scan/), [Redis CLI/TLS](https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/rs/references/cli-utilities/redis-cli/).
